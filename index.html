FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Fludd available now?

Not yet. It's in active development with a small group of pool companies. Join the early-access list and we'll bring you in as we open spots.

Do I have to move everything over at once?

No. Start by putting a few routes and customers in Fludd, then expand once it's working for you.

How is this different from Skimmer or Jobber?

Those focus on running your company, and Fludd does that too. The difference is that Fludd leads with the customer's experience — the part that keeps accounts from churning and drives referrals.

What will it cost?

Per pool or location, with unlimited users, priced under the incumbent and cheaper on annual billing. Exact rates are set at launch; early-access companies lock in founding pricing.

What about route optimization?

Included. Optimized daily routes from maps data, customer time windows, and live ETAs sent to homeowners. We treat it as a baseline, not the headline.

Who is Fludd for?

Pool service companies that want to keep the customers they have and win referrals without competing on price.
